### 📑 Detailed Section-by-Section Breakdown

#### 1. Atmospheric Time-of-Day System (`#bg-container`)
- **6 Scenic Atmospheric Skylines**:
  1. `🌅 ভোর (Early Morning / Bhor)`: Soft golden amber dawn over autumnal Bengal countryside.
  2. `☀️ সকাল (Morning / Sokal)`: Crisp golden Puja morning with azure sky and Kash flowers.
  3. `🌤️ দুপুর (Afternoon / Dupur)`: Warm midday autumn sunshine.
  4. `🌆 সন্ধ্যা (Evening / Sandhya)`: Glowing crimson and vermilion twilight suited for Aarti.
  5. `🏮 রাত (Night / Raat)`: Festive illumination with radiant warm lanterns.
  6. `✨ মধ্যরাত (Midnight / Modhyoraat)`: Deep starry serene obsidian night sky.
- **Dynamic Clock Sync (`auto`)**: Automatically detects local client hour and applies the matching sky.
- **Procedural Shiuli Flower Canvas (`#particle-canvas`)**: Organic 2D physics simulating delicate white and saffron Shiuli flowers drifting gently down the screen (Toggleable via Key `F`).

#### 2. Universal Floating Dynamic Island (`#universal-dynamic-island-container`)
- **Desktop & Mobile Universality**:
  - Desktop: Stays elegantly hovering at top center (`width: min(92vw, 440px)`).
  - Mobile: Sits neatly below status bar notch with fluid spring animations.
- **Dual State Machine**:
  - **State A (Idle)**: Sacred Diya (`🪔`), "মন্ডল বাড়ির পুজো", "১৫০+ বছর" badge, and live visitors beacon.
  - **State B (Active Audio)**: Spinning album art thumbnail, animated 3-bar equalizer, scrolling song title, and instant Play/Pause toggle.
- **Quick Action Hub Drawer**:
  - 5 Quick Trigger Pills: 🐚 শাঁখ, 🥁 ঢাক, 📻 রেডিও, 📸 স্টোরি, 🖼️ গ্যালারি (smoothly scrolls to gallery), 🎨 অন্যতা.
  - 6 Atmosphere Switcher Chips (`ভোর`, `সকাল`, `দুপুর`, `সন্ধ্যা`, `রাত`, `অটো`).
  - One-tap link to Mondal Bari 150+ Year Heritage Dossier.

#### 3. Hero Viewport Section (`#hero-section`)
- **Zero-Overlap Viewport Height**: Styled with `min-height: 100dvh; min-height: 100svh; height: auto;` so that elements expand fluidly on any screen without squashing or occluding neighboring components.
- **Authentic Calligraphy**: "পুজো আসছে" rendered in `Galada` with multi-layered text glow.
- **4-Unit Tabular Countdown Card**:
  - Live second-by-second countdown to **Maha Sasthi (16 October 2026)**.
  - `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate layout jitter.
  - Clicking the countdown card immediately opens the full 2026 Puja Schedule timeline.
- **Floating Sound Triggers**:
  - 🐚 শাঁখের আওয়াজ (Key `S`)
  - 🥁 ঢাকের বোল (Key `D`)
  - 📸 স্টোরি কার্ড (Key `I`)
